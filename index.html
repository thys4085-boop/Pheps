About

Hands-on, honest, mobile

Mahlangu Pheps Auto Repairs & Diagnostics is run by Mahlangu Pheps — a mobile mechanic who'd rather come to your car than have you tow it to a workshop. Every make, every model, old or new, diagnosed with proper scan tools and fixed by hand, on the spot where possible.

No waiting rooms, no marked-up parts you didn't ask for — just a straight diagnosis, a straight price, and a car that starts again.
